# Tax Strategist Skill

## Overview

Provides tax planning methodology, multi-jurisdictional compliance frameworks, transfer pricing guidance, and strategic tax optimization templates. Covers entity structuring, income timing, deduction maximization, and international tax architecture — with compliance as the floor and optimization as the mission.

## Capabilities

### Tax Planning Methodology

**5-Phase Tax Planning Process**
```
Phase 1 — Tax Position Assessment
  - Review current entity structure, historical returns, existing tax positions
  - Map all jurisdictional filing obligations and nexus exposures
  - Identify expiring elections, credits, and loss carryforwards
  - Assess transfer pricing policies and intercompany arrangements

Phase 2 — Opportunity Identification
  - Analyze ETR waterfall to identify optimization levers
  - Research available credits, incentives, and treaty benefits
  - Model alternative structures and their after-tax impact
  - Benchmark ETR against industry peers

Phase 3 — Strategy Development
  - Design recommended tax structures with implementation roadmaps
  - Prepare tax planning memos with authority analysis and risk assessment
  - Quantify expected savings with confidence ranges
  - Coordinate with legal counsel on structural changes

Phase 4 — Implementation & Compliance
  - Execute elections, filings, and structural changes on schedule
  - Prepare and review all required returns and disclosures
  - Maintain contemporaneous documentation for all positions
  - Monitor regulatory changes that impact existing strategies

Phase 5 — Ongoing Monitoring
  - Track ETR quarterly against targets
  - Update transfer pricing benchmarking studies annually
  - Monitor legislative and regulatory developments
  - Reassess strategies when business changes trigger tax implications
```

### Entity Structuring Framework

**Entity Selection Decision Tree**
```
Is the business generating profits?
  No → LLC (pass-through, loss utilization, flexibility)
  Yes:
    Will profits be reinvested or distributed?
      Reinvested → C-Corp (retained earnings taxed at 21%, QSBS eligible)
      Distributed → S-Corp / LLC (avoid double taxation)
    Is the business VC-fundable / IPO-track?
      Yes → C-Corp (preferred stock, 83(b) elections, option grants)
      No → LLC or S-Corp

International operations?
  Yes → Consider: IP holding entity (Netherlands, Ireland, Singapore)
        Transfer pricing requirements
        GILTI / FDII analysis
        Treaty benefits
```

**IRC Key Provisions Reference**
| Section | Description | Key Planning Opportunity |
|---------|-------------|------------------------|
| §83(b) | Property for services | 30-day election to convert ordinary income to capital gain |
| §1202 | QSBS exclusion | Up to $10M tax-free gain on qualified small business stock |
| §1045 | QSBS rollover | Defer gain by rolling into new QSBS within 60 days |
| §368 | Tax-free reorganizations | M&A transactions without immediate tax recognition |
| §355 | Spin-offs | Tax-free distribution of subsidiary stock |
| §199A | QBI deduction | Up to 20% deduction on qualified business income (pass-throughs) |
| §41 | R&D tax credit | Credit for increasing qualified research expenditures |
| §179 | Immediate expensing | Deduct full cost of qualifying property in year placed in service |
| §1031 | Like-kind exchanges | Defer capital gains on real property exchanges |

### Multi-Jurisdictional Compliance

**State & Local Tax (SALT) Nexus Analysis**
```
Physical Presence Nexus Triggers:
  - Employees working in state
  - Owned/leased property in state
  - Inventory stored in state (consignment, fulfillment centers)

Economic Nexus Triggers (post-Wayfair):
  - Sales exceeding $100K or 200 transactions in state (varies by state)
  - Digital products and services — check each state's specific rules

Nexus Determination Checklist:
  - [ ] Map all employee locations (remote workers create nexus)
  - [ ] Review all property leases and warehouse arrangements
  - [ ] Pull sales-by-state from revenue systems
  - [ ] Compare against each state's economic nexus thresholds
  - [ ] Register in all nexus states within 30 days of threshold breach
```

**International Tax Overview**
```
Key regimes:
  GILTI (Global Intangible Low-Taxed Income): 
    - Applies to CFCs with income above 10% QBAI return
    - Effective rate ~10.5% (2021), increasing to 15% (OECD Pillar Two)
    - FDII offset available for domestic IP with foreign sales

  Subpart F:
    - Currently taxable income for CFCs: passive income, base company sales/services income
    - No deferral; included in US parent income in year earned

  Foreign Tax Credits:
    - Dollar-for-dollar credit for foreign taxes paid
    - Basket limitations (passive, general, GILTI, FOGEI, FSOI)
    - Must manage baskets to avoid stranded FTCs

  Transfer Pricing:
    - Arm's length standard (IRC §482)
    - Methods: CUP, resale price, cost plus, TNMM, profit split
    - Annual benchmarking study required for intercompany transactions >$5M
    - Country-by-country reporting (Form 8975) for groups >$850M revenue
```

### Equity Compensation Tax Planning

**ISO vs. NSO Decision Framework**
| Feature | ISO | NSO |
|---------|-----|-----|
| Tax at grant | No | No |
| Tax at exercise | No (AMT preference) | Ordinary income on spread |
| Tax at sale | Capital gain (LTCG if held 2yr from grant, 1yr from exercise) | Capital gain on post-exercise appreciation |
| $100K limit | Yes (annual vesting) | No |
| Available to | Employees only | Employees + consultants |
| Employer deduction | None | Ordinary income amount |

**83(b) Election Mechanics**
```
Trigger: Receiving restricted stock (unvested property transferred for services)
Deadline: 30 days from date of transfer (IRS Form with statement)
Filing: IRS mailing + employer copy + keep personal copy
Effect: FMV at grant = ordinary income NOW; all future appreciation = capital gain
Best case: Grant price ≈ FMV (minimal income inclusion); equity grows to large LTCG
Risk: Pay tax on unvested shares; if forfeited, no refund (capital loss only)
```

## Scripts

### `scripts/etr_analyzer.py`

Calculates effective tax rate waterfall and identifies optimization opportunities.

```
Usage: python etr_analyzer.py tax_data.csv [--year 2025] [--benchmark-rate 0.21] [--format json|table]
Input CSV columns: component, amount, category (permanent/temporary/credit)
Output:
  - ETR waterfall from statutory rate to effective rate
  - Permanent vs. temporary difference breakdown
  - Year-over-year ETR comparison
  - Optimization opportunity scoring by effort/impact
```

### `scripts/nexus_analyzer.py`

Maps state nexus exposure 

```
Usage: python nexus_analyzer.py employees.csv sales_by_state.csv [--property property.csv] [--year 2025]
Input: Employee location CSV, sales-by-state CSV, optional property schedule
Output:
  - Nexus determination by state (physical / economic / both / none)
  - Registration requirement flags for unregistered nexus states
  - Exposure estimate for unregistered periods
  - Priority registration list by revenue exposure
```

### `scripts/equity_comp_tax_planner.py`

Models tax outcomes for equity compensation scenarios (ISO, NSO, RSU) across different exercise and sale timing strategies.

```
Usage: python equity_comp_tax_planner.py grants.csv [--ipo-date 2026-01-01] [--format json|table]
Input CSV columns: grant_type, grant_date, shares, strike_price, fmv_at_grant, vesting_schedule
Scenarios modeled:
  - Exercise and hold (ISO) vs. exercise and sell (NSO)
  - 83(b) election vs. standard vesting for restricted stock
  - AMT exposure calculation for ISO exercises
  - After-tax proceeds comparison across timing strategies
```

## Quality Standards

- ETR at or below industry peer median
- Zero penalties or interest from tax authorities
- 100% of returns filed on time across all jurisdictions
- All tax positions documented with contemporaneous memos
- Transfer pricing positions supported by current benchmarking studies
- Audit adjustments less than 2% of total tax liability
- Tax implications integrated into business decisions before execution
